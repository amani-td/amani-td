# 👋 Hi, I’m Amanuel T. Dufera (@amani-td)

## 🧑‍🔬 About Me
I am a robotics researcher at SUSTech in Shenzhen. My work covers how mobile robots and drones see, localize and move:
- **Visual and visual-inertial SLAM:** estimating a robot's pose and map from cameras and IMUs.
- **3D vision:** dense reconstruction and Gaussian-splatting scene representations.
- **Motion planning:** search- and sampling-based planners (A\*, kinodynamic A\*, RRT\*) and minimum-snap trajectories for UAVs.
- **Learned world models:** models that predict how a scene changes when a robot acts, so the robot can plan in their latent space.

## 🚀 Featured Projects

- [World-models](https://github.com/amani-td/World-models) — A reading list for joint-embedding predictive architectures (JEPA) and the world models built on them, from I-JEPA, V-JEPA 2 and LeJEPA to action-conditioned planners such as DINO-WM and LeWorldModel.
  - Also covers Hamiltonian, symplectic and Koopman latent dynamics, drone and robot applications, and the Dreamer-style models they are compared with.
  - Each paper links to its arXiv page, its peer-reviewed version where one exists, and its official code. Community reimplementations are marked separately.
  - A script checks arXiv every morning and adds new papers automatically.
- [vins-mono-ros2-jazzy](https://github.com/amani-td/vins-mono-ros2-jazzy) — VINS-Mono (monocular visual-inertial odometry with loop closure) ported to ROS 2 Jazzy on Ubuntu 24.04.
  - Moves to the new Ceres manifold API, updates ROS 2 includes and message types, and fixes leftover ROS 1 code.
  - The result builds on a current toolchain.
- [Caption-Generation](https://github.com/amani-td/Caption-Generation) — Image captioning with a pre-trained EfficientNetB0 encoder and a Transformer encoder-decoder for the text, in Keras/TensorFlow.
